# Contribution acceptance criteria

16 November 2026. Evaluator's working standard, following the strict novelty review. This does not freeze an experiment or authorize live calls. It records what must be demonstrated before a candidate earns substantial implementation or a submission slot. Intended users and benefits below are hypotheses to validate, not established demand.

The objective is up to two defensible contributions. Three candidates do not imply three full experiments, and two intended submissions do not justify submitting two weak studies. A well-designed, precise null can qualify; a dramatic effect with confounded measurement cannot.

## Gate 1 — establish a consequential question

Identify one intended user, one actual decision and one plausible failure pathway. Document how at least two possible findings would change that decision. A large number of affected animals or AI users establishes potential scale, not impact of this experiment.

For welfare projects, obtain independent feedback on a few representative cases before generating a large corpus. Ask whether the task occurs, whether available evidence resembles the proposed packets, and whether a better answer could affect action. Record objections and lack of demand. Do not turn expert comments into an endorsement they did not give.

**Acceptance:** a specific, source-grounded use case and a credible route from findings to a research or deployment decision. A benchmark resource can qualify if its measurement validity and unmet evaluation need are demonstrated; a new application need not invent a new algorithm.

## Gate 2 — establish a meaningful difference from prior work

Maintain a design-level comparison with the closest studies, including their actual outcome definitions and strongest controls. Specify one central contribution and the known-setting baseline/bridge required by the user's checklist. Baseline reproduction must be labeled separately from adaptation or conceptual replication.

Complete the independent 30-minute attack on the revised one-page proposal before substantial condition implementation. This timed step has not yet been completed. Resolve decisive objections in writing. Public-source searching cannot clear inaccessible unpublished work; disclose coverage and refresh positioning before submission.

**Acceptance:** the new comparison could teach something important beyond the predecessor. **Stop or reframe** if a duplicate is found and there is no justified replication, boundary, measurement or practical-transfer contribution. Existing code has no bearing on this judgment.

## Gate 3 — establish that the experiment measures the intended thing

P1 must repair the instruction confound and shared-initial-response failure handling, define the role of already-agreeing trials, and decide whether safeguards are baseline instructions or experimental treatments. Validate that pressure differs without introducing a request to verify in only one arm.

P2 must audit the entire prompt, including the appended probability model, for scope coaching. Separate scope-only disclosure from supplied conclusions; include facts-only outcome evidence and positive controls for warranted attribution under the proposed mitigation. Validate the distinction between belief and verification-attribution.

P3 must distinguish uncertain weights within a stipulated decision model from disagreement about that model. Include robust winners, ties and trade-off-dependent cases. Preserve counterexample policies for nonrobust cases. Have independent welfare-science and decision expertise check realism and the limits of the labels.

Across projects: analyze scheduled outcomes without selecting on checking or success; use appropriate item/family dependence; mask response extraction from gold status; retain technical failures; define what an inconclusive assay looks like. Software tests must cover the specified behavior, while human/expert validation covers constructs that tests cannot establish.

**Acceptance:** each main alternative explanation has a decisive control or an explicit limit on the claim. More trials cannot repair invalid measurement.

## Gate 4 — freeze a study capable of an informative result

Use a development pilot to diagnose manipulation, label ambiguity, failures and variance. Do not keep adjusting difficulty until a desired effect appears. Choose the smallest practically meaningful effects independently of observed pilot effects. Size by precision or power using the actual clustering and planned contrasts; document assumptions and sensitivity.

Before main results: freeze primary estimands, elementary test family, model selection, missingness rules, equivalence margins where relevant, substantive sampling and analysis. Create genuinely new held-out cases; inspected reserved fixtures are development material. Record revisions and an external timestamp. Confirmatory claims require this prospective separation.

**Acceptance:** positive, negative and mixed findings each have a defensible interpretation. A wide interval is inconclusive; a precise null is not failure. Resource limits may narrow the claim, but must not silently reduce necessary controls or precision.

## Gate 5 — earn a submission

An independent reviewer checks labels, raw-log-to-result reproduction and whether simpler explanations survive. The report states the strongest warranted claim, consequential limits, failed tests and deviations. Release materials sufficient for another team to inspect and reproduce the measurement, subject to data permissions.

Select projects on contribution and evidence quality, not statistical significance, the size of the failure rate, or agreement among AI agents. Keep a disposition record for all candidates and retain all main-run findings. Do not hide inconvenient nulls when selecting the final two.

**Acceptance:** the result changes understanding, validates a needed measurement, tests a consequential boundary or supports a useful intervention. Publication and downstream welfare benefits cannot be guaranteed in advance.
