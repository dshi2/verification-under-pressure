# §2.3 Execution discipline — reviewed draft

Status: evaluator's proposed replacement for the pasted checklist. Not a protocol freeze, permission for live calls, or verification of externally named files. Apply after contribution review, with a diagnostic freeze before a pilot and a separate confirmatory freeze after revisions.

**Gate question:** Is this attempted observation part of an authorized, versioned plan, and does its configuration satisfy the invariants for its intended comparison?

1. **Freeze the planned design.** Record item/block, condition, model/configuration, prompt version, repeat, allocation and run phase. Enumerate planned observations or preserve a deterministic generator and its immutable inputs. Reject unauthorized cells and duplicate attempts unless the retry policy permits them.
2. **Specify allocation rather than demand universal balance.** Use equal allocation where appropriate. Justify unequal allocation, incomplete crossing or model-specific subsets prospectively. Define which contrasts each subset supports. Audit planned counts separately from attempted, completed and scorable counts.
3. **Declare contrast-specific invariants.** For each main contrast, list the intended intervention and what must remain fixed: starting history, information, permissions, instructions, tool opportunities and resource policies. An intervention can deliberately change turns or access; it must then be interpreted as that workflow change. Interaction claims require their specified factorial comparisons.
4. **Resolve shared starting states before branching.** Cache the same genuine initial response for matched branches. Apply a prospective failure policy to the shared state; do not let one branch fail initial generation while another silently retries it. Persist the starting-state identity and outcome.
5. **Specify stopping and retry policies.** Fix turn caps or stopping rules, distinguishing allowed opportunities from realized actions. No outcome-driven extension or selective rerun. Classify provider/transport failures, model refusals, malformed responses and scoring failures separately. If retries are part of the deployed workflow, evaluate that declared policy and retain every attempt.
6. **Check feasibility and precision before the main run.** Cost the planned calls, including shared initialization and permitted retries, against an enforced spending cap. Set independent case/block counts from justified precision or power targets and clustering assumptions. Repeats do not create independent cases. If resources are insufficient, revise before execution; if the cap halts a run, preserve and report the incomplete experiment.
7. **Keep pilot and confirmation distinct.** Pilot observations are real model observations, but remain development evidence under this design. Do not silently pool them into confirmation. Document revisions, freeze again and use genuinely new confirmatory cases. A null pilot is not a reason to search for a prompt that produces failures.
8. **Randomize or block execution appropriately.** Interleave conditions where needed to limit confounding with time/provider changes. Preserve assignment and order. Account for shared-cache dependencies; do not randomize in a way that breaks the required starting-state construction.
9. **Log complete manifests and raw observations.** Record code, treatment and scoring hashes, planned-design identity, exact requests/results, provider/model identifiers, returned version metadata, parameters, dates, repeat IDs, failures and retries. If an immutable provider version cannot be pinned, disclose the alias and collection window. A seed is not a guarantee of deterministic provider behavior.
10. **Validate manifest compatibility, not byte equality.** Model, item, treatment, timestamp and repeat fields may legitimately differ. Define analysis grouping and compatibility rules prospectively. Reject undocumented differences that invalidate a comparison; retain and disclose the affected observations rather than silently erasing them.
11. **Version changes according to their scientific effect.** Treatment, case-population, model or outcome changes create a new analysis group unless a prospective pooling rule justifies combination. A reporting typo or audited implementation repair does not automatically require every model call again. Identify affected observations and rerun the complete affected comparison when necessary, independent of whether its results are favorable. Retain all versions and deviations.
12. **Check the executed design before inference.** Produce planned/attempted/completed/scorable counts and failure types by cell/model/block. Unexpected imbalance triggers diagnosis and the prespecified missingness analysis, not automatic deletion or refusal to examine results. Condition-dependent refusals may themselves be outcomes. Stop affected causal claims when required comparability fails; continue descriptive accounting of what occurred.
13. **Validate analysis before main outcomes.** Use known-null and known-effect synthetic data, including clustered repeats, unequal counts and failures. Prepare table shells and exact outcome denominators. Label primary, secondary and exploratory analyses in code. Apply the declared multiplicity policy; exploratory results must not become definitive claims merely because they were exempted from adjustment.
14. **Control amendments and claims.** New ideas enter a separate versioned study or explicitly exploratory analysis. Do not tune a running confirmatory treatment. Independently reconcile the executed plan and reproduce the results before publication. Distinguish engineering approval from scientific and publication approval.

## Required report

| Field | What the audit must establish |
|---|---|
| Planned allocation | Every intended cell and justified omission is known. |
| Actual exposure | Every attempt and its evidence/action opportunities are traceable. |
| Missingness | Infrastructure failures, refusals and unscorable outputs remain visible by condition. |
| Comparability | Each contrast satisfies its declared invariants or has a documented limitation. |
| Versions | Included observations meet explicit compatibility rules. |
| Analysis | Denominators, clustering and missingness follow the frozen plan. |

Equal counts cannot establish comparability on their own. Unequal observed counts do not, on their own, destroy it.
