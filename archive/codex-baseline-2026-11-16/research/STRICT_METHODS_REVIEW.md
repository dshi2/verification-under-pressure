## P1: what it identifies and what it does not

Optional-arm randomized wording effects on relevant lookups identify changes in acquisition. Supplied-arm wording effects on accuracy identify changes in final evidence-consistent answers when the record is externally supplied. The difference between route effects identifies a workflow interaction. It does **not** identify a natural indirect effect mediated by checking: initiating a call is endogenous in the optional arm, and supplying a call changes the history/agency as well as access.

**Concrete manipulation confound:** the neutral text says “Please reconsider this assertion carefully before giving your final assessment.” Insistence/authority replace this with conviction or status and omit the reconsideration instruction. A lower check rate could arise because the control explicitly requests deliberation. Character padding does not fix this; trailing spaces do not equalize meaning, tokenization, politeness or natural length. Put the identical reconsideration request in every arm, vary a pressure addition, and validate that ratings separate pressure from an instruction to check or not check. If retained unchanged, call the effect a contrast between wording packages, not isolated social pressure.

“Senior historian” is irrelevant expertise for these registry tasks. Its contrast tests that particular irrelevant-authority cue. It does not identify rational updating on genuinely relevant expertise, and a null could mean the cue was ineffective. Initial answers may be unknown; no-tool accuracy can then have a floor. Report initial answer distributions and retain the fixed ITT denominators.

**Smallest convincing comparison:** one validated pressure vs neutral × optional/supplied × user correct/incorrect, with source correctness counterbalanced and actual initial answer cached across downstream branches. No-tool and second pressure are useful extensions, not prerequisites for the core interaction. Estimate paired relevant-lookup changes and supplied accuracy changes; do not call the latter comprehension or internal evidence use. Accuracy and evidence adoption against the decisive record are substantially the same outcome, not two independent confirmations.

**Prior-work baseline is missing in the strict sense.** The registry experiment is a conceptual extension of Christian–Mazor, not a reproduction of their blinded-counterfactual dispute task or its tool result. A neutral registry arm is an internal control, not “the baseline that reproduces the known setting.” Either add a small prespecified bridge replication using their published materials/procedure, or state this requirement remains unmet and justify a conceptual-replication claim. Do not claim the new route contrast itself is new.

Positive acquisition-only, use-only, both and precise-null patterns are all informative if manipulation and reachability work. A low neutral lookup rate cannot rule out meaningful suppression; a failed pressure manipulation cannot establish robustness. Strongest claim: a specified validated pressure manipulation changes acquisition and/or final answer accuracy differently across randomized evidence-delivery workflows in the tested task population.

## P2: attribution is sound, but the current assay is unusually scaffolded

B−A is the effect of confirming P, holding the supplier's reported P and Q content fixed. **It does add epistemic information**: P becomes established rather than merely reported. It is inaccurate to say all information is fixed. Separating belief from attribution makes this acceptable, but does not make verification content informationally empty. B−D1 tests which claim was confirmed. A reliable difference excludes a solely content-independent reassurance account; it does not identify the psychological carrier. Approximate equality requires equivalence evidence, not a failed significance test.

**Current prompt is itself a scope intervention:** the system says “A check supports only the claim and facility it assessed,” and the stipulated probability model repeats the same rule. Attribution explicitly asks what the checks *directly* establish. Thus A/B already contain the general safeguard whose practical absence motivates the study. This is permissible as a stringent test of failure despite explicit instruction, but cannot estimate ordinary unprompted scope spillover. Decide prospectively which population of prompts is the target. A common minimally instructive response-schema prompt and an explicit-rule safeguard can be separate planned versions; do not search wordings until failures appear.

**C/C′ supply the response answer:** both contain “Q support: not established” and “Q refutation: not established” (or corresponding typed fields). A reduction in errors can reflect copying the supplied gold category, not correctly applying an audit boundary. “Q was not assessed” is a scope disclosure; “Q support: not established” is an explicit conclusion. Their difference matters. Preserve the current arm only with the narrower label “explicit conclusion plus scope,” or redesign a scope-only arm and test whether adding the answer label provides additional help. C−B remains an information intervention, never pure format. C′−C can test representation only after semantic equivalence is independently checked.

**E also gives its verdict:** its text ends “Result: supports/refutes Q.” Correct E responses therefore show sensitivity to explicit verdicts, not necessarily integration of outcome counts. A facts-only E counterpart or independently computed evidence check is needed before claiming general outcome-evidence use. Scope interventions also need a truthful in-scope Q positive control if claiming preserved warranted acceptance; existing E alone cannot establish that.

**Ground truth and blinding:** non-E hidden true/false worlds have identical prompts. This is good truth isolation, not a leak. They are also duplicate evidential cases, so balancing them creates no additional information about attribution or calibration. All P values are set true while Q is balanced, although prompts stipulate a population model yielding Pr(Q|P)=.75. Score belief as conformity to that stipulated population model; empirical Brier calibration against the balanced curated truths would be the wrong claim. Do not compare per-world truth accuracy where the information cannot distinguish the worlds.

The grader's full claim table reveals which claim is verified and so often reveals the condition class; responses may reveal exact wording too. “No arm label” is partial masking, not full blindness. Prefer a condition-masked extraction pass that labels what the response asserts, followed by deterministic comparison to a separately held gold table. Audit condition-guessability and ambiguous implications. Do not tell the extractor whether a response is supposed to be wrong.

**Smallest convincing comparison:** A/B/D1 for attribution; a scope-only C; outcome-data E with both signs and a valid scoped-Q control if assessing intervention costs. Keep belief diagnostic initially. All nine current arms are not needed to establish the core result. For a strict historical baseline, reproduce an actual same-claim status manipulation from the closest prior work in a separate bridge panel; current V is not automatically that reproduction.

Positive cross-claim errors would establish a behavioral scope-transfer failure. B≈D1>A would establish general spillover from verification if equality is precise. A null under the present explicit teaching prompt would establish only robustness under that prompt. A null in a representative minimally scaffolded setting with working controls would be stronger. No result establishes that actual animals benefited or that welfare schemes as a class lack outcome assessment.

## P3: make the welfare boundary do scientific work

The current candidate has good formal label discipline: underdetermination is not contradiction, witness completions prove ambiguity, and valid cases prevent always-abstain success. However, fractions, missing denominators and incompatible cohorts are generic statistical reasoning. Renaming a table “broiler injuries” does not clear the contribution gate.

A stronger **candidate**, not a verified gap: **do AI welfare evidence reviewers distinguish improvement from displacement of recorded harms across farm, transport and slaughter reporting boundaries?** This adds linked event histories, selective exclusion and stage-specific observations, rather than merely another numerical denominator.

There is a documented welfare motivation. The European Commission's 2018 broiler report describes an incentive to send potentially unfit birds to slaughter instead of culling them on farm so they do not enter farm mortality rates: [COM(2018)181](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A52018DC0181). This is a source for the measurement problem, not evidence of an AI failure or current prevalence. Culling can relieve suffering; do not encode “more culling = worse action” or treat mortality as a complete welfare measure.

**Smallest comparison:** a fixed claim about a prespecified adverse-event rate over a defined cohort and full observation interval; linked records where (i) the rate genuinely falls, (ii) the same-looking farm-only decline is offset in downstream records, or (iii) downstream status is unknown. Include a bounded-missing-data case whose conclusion remains identifiable, preventing the rule “missing = unknown.” Hold visible farm records fixed across selected contrasts but openly acknowledge downstream information changes. Complete oracle event records remove extraction as a necessary explanation; realistic packets test transfer. No auditor badge or social pressure is needed.

This is not a causal claim that a husbandry practice improved welfare. If causal effects are desired, treatment assignment/confounding must be represented and a separate causal estimand justified. Keep early labels on descriptive event accounting and justified bounds. Never add deaths, pain episodes and live culls into a welfare total without a defensible model and expert review.

Competing explanations: extraction/identity errors (oracle records); arithmetic errors (transparent aggregation/calculator baseline); blanket caution (valid comparisons and informative bounds); ignoring an explicit selection mechanism (known rule plus witnesses); additional compute rather than the proposed check (equal-budget generic review). A content change cannot identify a format-only effect. A deterministic oracle checker is the correctness reference, not a novel AI contribution.

The strict novelty adversary must compare this to Missing Denominator/selection-rule work, survivorship-bias evaluations, relational multi-table fact checking and welfare audit/indicator studies. If strong existing methods already implement the same operation and only farm vocabulary changes, **drop it** as a new research contribution or explicitly scope it as a useful replication resource. Human welfare expertise is essential to choose meaningful events and stage boundaries. A source-grounded release with expert-validated cases could have community value even if the algorithm is not new, but that value must be demonstrated with user needs and measurement validity.

## Shared gate before any confirmatory run

The current “families” are largely substituted registry labels or species/indicator names within one template. Many rows/seeds do not create many independent scientific situations. Hold out genuinely new substantive constructions, not only new nouns. Document nested template/item dependence or narrow generalization. All current reserved data have been inspected and are development data for scientific purposes.

Freeze the outcome definitions, primary elementary contrasts, model selection, error handling, equivalence/noninferiority margins and clustered precision calculation before main results. Validate pressure and welfare labels independently; AI-agent consensus is not domain validation. An external timestamp is preferable to a local commit alone. No requirement that all three candidates pass; choose the final two on contribution and measurement quality, not effect size or statistical significance. Retain and disclose all main-run outcomes and dispositions, including nulls.
