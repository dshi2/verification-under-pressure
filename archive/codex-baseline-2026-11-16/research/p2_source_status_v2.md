# P2 revision 2: primary-source access status

Checked 2026-11-16 through direct web opens of the four exact URLs in `p2.py`, followed by targeted text inspection. All four were accessible. These sources ground indicator choice and evidence-level distinctions only. They do **not** supply or validate the fictional facility facts, space allocations, before/after counts, truth labels, reliability assumptions, or Bayesian probabilities.

| Code source | Access and inspected location | What it supports; limits |
|---|---|---|
| [WOAH, 2021 Terrestrial Code, Chapter 7.10](https://www.woah.org/fileadmin/Home/eng/Health_standards/tahc/2021/en_chapitre_aw_broiler_chicken.htm) | Direct HTML opened; Article 7.10.3 text inspected, including outcome-based measures and the mortality, gait and contact-dermatitis subsections. | Broiler welfare assessment can use animal-based measures, including mortality, gait abnormalities, dermatitis and injuries. Appropriate thresholds depend on the setting. This is the specified **2021 edition**, not a claim about the newest standard; no source-defined threshold was imported into the synthetic cases. |
| [EFSA, Welfare of broilers on farm](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plain-language-summary/welfare-broilers-farm) | Direct official HTML opened; dated 21 February 2023. The animal-based-measure recommendation and key implications inspected. Access depth: **official plain-language summary**, not full scientific opinion or underlying studies. | Names wounds, carcass condemnation, mortality and footpad dermatitis as relevant indicators monitored at slaughter. Supports the contextual choice of wound and condemnation indicators. It does not validate our generic per-100-observation protocol or attribute outcome change to space alone. |
| [RSPCA welfare standards for laying hens](https://www.rspca.org.uk/documents/d/rspca/rspca-welfare-standards-for-laying-hens) | Direct 114-page PDF opened; extracted text inspected from July 2025 edition, WA 1.3–1.4 at printed page 65, and perch/aggression discussion at printed pages 23 and 97. Not a full-document review; embedded scoresheet graphics were not independently transcribed. | Supports keeping time, houses and assessed population explicit; the document warns that outcome assessment does not always estimate definitive farm prevalence. It also discusses feather loss and aggression. Does not establish that a practice check alone measures those outcomes, or validate our invented thresholds. |
| [RSPCA Science, Welfare outcome assessment](https://science.rspca.org.uk/sciencegroup/farmanimals/outcomeassessment) | Direct HTML opened; introduction, “Where we are now,” and “The benefits” inspected. Page contains historical project language and is undated; copyright year is not a publication date. | Explicitly distinguishes resources from direct animal condition/behavior assessment. Names feather cover/aggression for hens and lameness/body condition for dairy cattle, and says outcome assessments inform compliance checks. Supports the dairy indicator choices and the fact that audits **can assess outcomes**. Not a current inventory of all scheme requirements or an impact estimate. |

No access failure occurred for these four links. No field expert reviewed the synthetic cases. The report makes no claim that the sources endorse the experiment, that the mock observations follow a validated veterinary scoring protocol, or that observed before/after improvement identifies a causal benefit.
